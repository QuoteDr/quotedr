Hey, here is what changed in QuoteDr lately. I am keeping these updates practical: what got better, why it matters on a real job, and how it saves quoting time.

Quoting

Legacy Quote Import

The old-quote importer is now in beta. It can turn older PDF, Excel, CSV, or pasted quote text into QuoteDr rooms and line items, with review before anything gets applied.

Try it from Quote Builder under Tools, then review the preview before applying it.

Client Portal

Change Orders Are Taking Shape

Change orders are being built into the QuoteDr workflow so extra work can be priced, reviewed, and documented in the client portal.

Use change orders to keep extra work documented instead of buried in texts.

Client Experience

Client Portal Improvements

The client portal keeps getting cleaner: better review flow, clearer client-facing totals, and a stronger place for approvals and job documents.

Send client links when you want the quote to feel easier to review than a static PDF.

Invoicing

Invoice Payment Options

Invoices now support more realistic payment options: e-transfer instructions, cheques, cash, and Stripe when the account is connected.

Add e-transfer and cheque details in settings so clients know how to pay.

Quoting tip

Detailed quotes sell better because clients can see what they are buying. QuoteDr is built to make that level of detail fast enough that you are not gambling your whole evening on one quote.
