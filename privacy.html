4. AI Voice Transcripts and Optional Audio

When you submit text from Voice To Quote using Generate Quote, QuoteDr saves that text transcript to your authenticated account. We retain it so you can review what was submitted, connect a later question to the source wording, and help us investigate a reported AI Voice problem.

  • Audio requires an explicit first-use choice: Before Voice To Quote can use the microphone for the first time, you must choose either Save private audio for 14 days or Transcript only. QuoteDr does not begin recording before that decision. Your selection is saved as your account default, shown whenever you open Voice To Quote, and can be changed later. Choosing Transcript only prevents QuoteDr from retaining original microphone audio; it does not silently delete recordings already listed in your history.
  • Separate records: Text transcript retention is separate from optional audio. Audio expiry or deletion does not delete, shorten, or overwrite the text transcript. The optional audio is support evidence and is not sent to the quote parser.
  • Private storage and playback: Audio files are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket, not in the public database and not at a public URL. Playback uses a short-lived access link after an ownership or authorized-support check. QuoteDr does not place a service-role credential in your browser.
  • Normal audio retention: Optional audio is scheduled for automatic deletion from active storage 14 days after capture. Each history entry shows its file size and current expiry date. Browser formats and encoded sizes vary; QuoteDr enforces per-recording duration and file-size limits and a rolling account storage cap.
  • Case-specific preservation: Before normal expiry, you can preserve one recording for a specific support case by entering the case reference, explaining the reason, and explicitly authorizing support access. An active authorized case pauses normal expiry. When you or authorized support closes the case hold, the recording is scheduled for deletion after a 30-day post-case period.
  • Your deletion control: You can delete optional audio immediately unless that exact recording is temporarily held for an active case you authorized. The history explains an active hold and lets you end it; after the hold ends, you may delete the recording immediately.
  • Account access: Other customers cannot view your transcripts. You can view, copy, or delete them from AI Voice Transcript History.
  • AI processing: The submitted text is sent to our AI service provider to create and verify the quote draft.
  • Limited, audited support review: QuoteDr staff do not routinely view transcripts. QuoteDr staff do not routinely access or listen to recordings. Transcript review requires an exact account identity and a real case or investigation reason. Authorized support may listen only after you explicitly authorize the exact recording for a specific customer-support investigation and case. Audio cannot be browsed generally: support must use the exact account, exact active case reference, an investigation reason, and the owner's explicit recording authorization. Access is case-bound and audited. Transcript searches, playback, case holds, deletion, and cleanup are audited; access-audit records are retained for up to two years for security and accountability.
  • Sensitive information: Avoid dictating access codes or unnecessary sensitive information. Voice To Quote includes a visible live recording indicator and a pause control so you can pause during private conversations.
  • Investigation evidence only: A stored recording can help a human compare what was said with the transcript and generated draft. It is not represented as legal proof and does not automatically guarantee compensation, a credit, refund, or reimbursement.
  • No advertising use: QuoteDr does not sell Voice To Quote transcripts or use them for advertising or routine employee monitoring.
  • Text retention: Transcripts remain in your active account until you delete them or the account is removed, subject to legal obligations and normal backup-protection periods.
