Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our Service:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, business name, and address
  • Account Information: Username, password, subscription details, billing information
  • Business Data: Quotes, invoices, customer information, payment history
  • Usage Data: Browser and device type, pages viewed, referral domain, usage patterns, and approximate city, region, and country derived from an internet address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send invoices, quotes, and transactional communications
  • Improve, test, and develop our Service
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Send service updates and promotional communications (with your consent)

Important: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share your data with service providers necessary to operate the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on Supabase, a PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption at rest and in transit
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Backup and disaster recovery procedures

4. AI Voice Transcripts

When you submit text from Voice To Quote using Generate Quote, QuoteDr saves that text transcript to your authenticated account. We retain it so you can review what was submitted, connect a later question to the source wording, and help us investigate a reported AI Voice problem.

  • No stored microphone audio: Your browser or device speech service may process microphone audio to create the transcript. QuoteDr stores the submitted text returned by that service, not the microphone recording.
  • Account access: Other customers cannot view your transcripts. You can view, copy, or delete them from AI Voice Transcript History.
  • AI processing: The submitted text is sent to our AI service provider to create and verify the quote draft.
  • Limited support review: QuoteDr staff do not routinely view your transcripts. Authorized support personnel may access one only when you ask us to investigate a reported AI Voice error, billing dispute, or possible billing adjustment or other support resolution. Support access requires a case or investigation reason and is logged; those access-audit records are retained for up to two years for security and accountability.
  • No advertising use: QuoteDr does not sell Voice To Quote transcripts or use them for advertising or routine employee monitoring.
  • Retention: Transcripts remain in your active account until you delete them or the account is removed, subject to legal obligations and normal backup-protection periods.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Service:

  • Stripe: Payment processing and billing
  • Supabase: Database hosting and management
  • Cloudflare: Website hosting and security
  • OpenAI: AI processing for features such as Voice To Quote
  • PostHog: Privacy-conscious website and product analytics, including coarse geographic and referral reporting

These third parties have access to your data only to perform the services they provide and are prohibited from using it for other purposes.

Our administrator traffic view uses generated visitor labels and approximate location only. It does not display or copy raw IP addresses, exact coordinates, quote contents, client information, material details, or pricing data into visitor alerts.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies to enhance your experience:

  • Session Cookies: Required for you to use the Service
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand which public pages and product tools are useful (optional and disabled when browser Do Not Track or QuoteDr analytics opt-out is active)

Marketing analytics use normalized page routes without query strings, hashes, portal tokens, client identifiers, or full referral URLs. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.
