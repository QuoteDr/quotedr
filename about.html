Built from the job site... literally

QuoteDr exists because quoting should not eat your weekend.

It started as a way to replace a contractor's spreadsheet with something faster, cleaner, and easier for clients to say yes to.

Made by someone who has actually quoted the work.

QuoteDr was built by Adam, a service contractor in Burlington, Ontario. It was not dreamed up by suits in an office or tech people guessing what contractors need. It was built by someone who has quoted real jobs, lost evenings to spreadsheets, and understands the tools and workflow contractors actually need. The goal was simple: keep the detailed quoting power contractors need, but remove the copy-paste, formatting, recalculating, and follow-up chaos that slows everything down.

Most contractors already know their trade. What they need is a quoting system that remembers their common rooms, line items, upgrades, terms, invoice details, and client communications. QuoteDr is built around that practical workflow.

QuoteDr is designed first for solo operators and small crews who need professional client-facing quotes without enterprise software prices. It is also expanding quickly, with new tools, smarter workflows, and better job tracking methods being added over time. Follow the early-access launch to grow with the platform as those contractor-focused tools keep improving.

What QuoteDr cares about

  • Fast change orders that protect the contractor and client when the scope changes.
  • Quotes that clients can understand on their phone.
  • Reusable pricing without losing contractor judgment.
  • Invoices and payment details that represent the user's business.
  • Fair pricing for small service businesses.
  • Detailed quotes that are fast enough to send without gambling your weekend.
  • Tools that save time on real jobs, not just look good in demos.
