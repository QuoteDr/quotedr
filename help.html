How-To Manuals

These quick manuals match the Help buttons inside QuoteDr windows. Video walkthroughs can be added here when they are ready.

Start from the dashboard, create a quote with the client name, then open the builder. Add rooms first, then add line items inside each room. Save before leaving the page.

Use room or area breaks to organize the job. Add line items with a clear description, unit type, quantity, and rate. Review room subtotals and the quote total before sending.

Manage Line Items is your saved price list. Keep item names searchable, set the rate you charge, and optionally track material cost and supplier links for margin review. Use Manage Items > Choice Group to create saved client option groups from your saved items. Pick One is for one selected material or option; Pick Multiple is for add-ons where the client can choose several. Auto-grouping can automatically use a saved group when one of its items is added to a quote.

Use Send Quote Settings to choose the client view, approval options, expiry, deposit display, and message. Preview the client link before sending it by email or text.

Satellite Measure, Floor Plan Scanner, and Material Estimator help turn dimensions into quote quantities. Always review calculated quantities before adding them to a client quote.

Flooring, paint, and drywall calculators estimate material quantities from dimensions or scanned quote data. Adjust waste, coverage, sheet size, and other assumptions to match the real job.

Specialty tools such as IKEA Quick Quote speed up repeated workflows. Set your pricing first, parse or enter the source information, review the results, then add them to the correct room.

AI Quote Builder works best when you describe the job clearly and your saved pricing list is current. Always use AI Voice Review to check parsed items, uncertain matches, duplicates, learned mappings, saved groups, and room scope notes before sending to a client. AI Memory remembers phrase corrections, AI Trade Rules apply contractor shorthand, and Voice Templates apply repeatable packages.

Save client details to reduce typing and avoid delivery mistakes. Use the portal link and PIN when clients need secure access to their quote history or project information.

Generate invoices from completed quote information, then review the total before sending. In Settings > Payments, Stripe can show a deposit payment button on quote links and a pay-in-full button on invoice links. Use deposit tools and Stripe checkout only after payment settings are ready.

Getting Started

Log in, click "New Quote" from the dashboard, fill in your client's name and project details, then add rooms and line items. You can type items manually or use the AI voice feature to speak your quote out loud.

Go to Settings → Business Profile. Fill in your name, address, HST number, and tagline. This info appears automatically on every quote you send.

QuoteDr works on any device with a browser — iPhone, Android, iPad, laptop, or desktop. The voice quote feature works best on iPhone/Safari.
