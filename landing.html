AI Voice to Quote learns how you sell the job.

Instead of starting from a blank quote, the user can speak naturally: what the client wants, the rooms involved, materials, options, exclusions, and the rough order of work. QuoteDr turns that into a usable quote structure, then the contractor can edit anything before it goes to the client.

Custom wordingIt can be tuned around the user's language, saved items, services, and preferred quote style, so the output feels like their business instead of generic AI copy.
Gets faster over timeSaved items, repeated rooms, templates, and edited descriptions give the user a stronger starting point each time they quote similar work.
Big time saverA messy job conversation can become rooms, scope, notes, and line items in minutes, then the contractor just reviews and adjusts.

Floor Plan Scanner turns takeoff work into quote structure.

The user can upload a floor plan or sketch and move quickly into rooms, quantities, and quote-ready work areas. It is built to be lightweight and accurate: the user enters the measurement of an existing line on the scaled drawing, and the tool calibrates from there. No AI guessing. It is built for contractors who quote from drawings, sketches, plans, or layout files and want the estimate organized faster.

Less manual measuringUse plan details to speed up room creation, quantities, and measurements that would normally require a long takeoff session.
Room-by-room outputThe quote can stay organized by room or area, which makes it easier for the client to understand and easier for the contractor to check.
Easy to reviewThe scanner gives the contractor a starting point, not a locked result. They can adjust quantities, rates, notes, and scope before sending.

IKEA Kitchen Quoter handles the cabinet-list grind.

IKEA kitchen quotes can take an entire evening because every cabinet, panel, filler, drawer, and accessory needs to be understood and priced. QuoteDr can read the order PDF or pasted list, itemize it, apply the user's install pricing, and add it to the quote fast. If the user is charging IKEA kitchens by linear footage alone, complex kitchens with lots of inner fittings can quietly burn their margin. This breaks every item down individually in seconds, so a simple kitchen can cost less than a complex kitchen with many parts. Professional, fair, and much easier to defend.

Upload or pasteDrop in the IKEA order PDF or paste the order list, then let QuoteDr parse the package into quote-ready items.
Starter pricing includedIndustry-standard install pricing is built in so line items already have a starting price. The user can adjust those rates and save their own pricing so the quote reflects how they actually charge.
Seconds, not eveningsWhat used to be repetitive cabinet counting and spreadsheet cleanup becomes a quick review-and-add workflow.

Built for every service business

Why We Built This

I'm Adam, a renovation and service contractor in Burlington Ontario. For years, I quoted jobs out of Excel. Honestly? My spreadsheets looked great! I built up a great template over the years. Clients loved the detail. But a big quote could eat an entire day...or a few! Copy, paste, calculate, re-format, double-check, repeat. Staring at a screen when I could be on a job site.

I needed my time back. So I went looking for software. Sat through demos with the big names in the industry — slick products, sharp salespeople. But $300 a month? $800 a month as you grow? For features I'll never use? And basic stuff like client payment reminders locked behind a premium tier? No thanks.

So I built my own. QuoteDr started as a tool for me. Now it's for every service professional who's ever lost a Saturday to a spreadsheet, or sent a quote that looked like it was made in 1998.

Made by a service contractor. For the solo operator, the two-person crew, the small team that just wants their time back — and quotes that actually win jobs!

— Adam, Founder & Contractor
